Q & A

Frequently
asked.

Do you work with small businesses?
Yes — in fact, most of our clients are small to medium businesses and founders. We love working with brands that are ambitious but need the right strategy to scale their social presence effectively.
What platforms do you cover?
We cover all major platforms — Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube Shorts and WeChat. Our full-platform matrix approach ensures your content is adapted for each channel, not just repurposed.
How does pricing work?
We offer both project-based and monthly retainer pricing. Every engagement starts with a strategy consultation so we can scope exactly what you need — no packages that don't fit.
How quickly can we start?
Usually within 2 weeks of confirming scope. We begin with a kick-off session to deep-dive your brand, audience and goals before building anything.
Will I work with you directly?
Always. You work directly with the Studio Director — no account managers, no junior handoffs. This is a boutique studio by design, and that direct relationship is central to how we work.
What is brand merchandise sourcing?
We have direct international procurement channels for custom branded merchandise — ideal for brand activations, campaign giveaways or product launches. We handle sourcing, customisation and logistics.
