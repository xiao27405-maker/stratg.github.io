05 / FAQ

Frequently
asked.

Do you work with small businesses?
+
Yes — most of our clients are small to medium businesses and founders. We're specifically built for businesses that are ambitious but need the right system to grow their presence effectively, without the cost or complexity of a large agency.
What platforms do you cover?
+
Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, and WeChat. We adapt content for each channel's native format — not just repurpose the same post everywhere.
How does pricing work?
+
We offer both project-based and monthly retainer structures. Every engagement starts with a free consultation to scope exactly what you need. For brand sourcing, we charge 10% of the total order value — you get the rest of the savings vs local suppliers.
Will I work directly with you?
+
Always. You work directly with the Studio Director — no account managers, no junior handoffs. This is a boutique studio by design, and that direct relationship is central to how we work and why results are consistent.
How quickly can we start?
+
Usually within 2 weeks of confirming scope. We begin with a kick-off session to understand your brand, audience, and goals before building anything.
What exactly is the brand sourcing service?
+
We have direct access to trusted manufacturers in China. We source, sample, quality-check, and coordinate logistics for custom branded merchandise — merch, packaging, promotional items. We charge 10% of total order value. For most clients, even with our fee included, the total cost is 40–60% less than sourcing locally.
