Behind The Studio

The brand story.

No time. No spare budget. No idea where to even start. That's the reality for most small business owners — not a lack of ambition, just a lack of a system. StratG exists to fix that: one studio that plans it, makes it, posts it, and even sources the product behind it — so growth stops depending on you finding another hour in the day.

The founder story.

Galina has spent 7+ years working inside the industries small businesses actually come from — education, hospitality, beauty, events and banquets, floristry, modelling, bars and cafés. That's what shapes the strategy: not theory, but knowing how these businesses really run, what their customers respond to, and where the budget actually needs to go. On the sourcing side, that same hands-on approach means working directly with factories in China — no middlemen — so quality stays consistent and the price stays fair. When you work with StratG, you work with Galina, always.
